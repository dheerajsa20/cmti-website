CMTI Finishing School

Certificate Course on Advanced Construction Management

3 months · Offline, online and hybrid · Hands-on exercises and construction site visits

Enquire about the course

Who should apply?

Eligibility

Diploma holders, graduates and postgraduates, including freshers and young professionals with one to two years of experience.

Target audience

Aspiring construction professionals who want practical skills in site execution, quantity estimation, quality and safety. The course also supports those preparing for job interviews or exploring construction entrepreneurship.

Watch on YouTube →

Programme objective

Prepare participants for day-to-day site responsibilities by building their ability to interpret drawings and specifications, understand construction materials, perform calculations, estimate quantities and costs, and sequence construction activities.

Learning outcomes

  • Interpret drawings and specifications, convert units, and take site measurements.
  • Identify cement, aggregates, reinforcement steel and masonry units, and explain their properties, uses and basic quality checks.
  • Calculate area, volume, material quantities and reinforcement weight.
  • Prepare Bills of Quantities (BOQs), Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) and rate analyses.
  • Sequence construction activities and explain where formwork, construction methods and site execution procedures fit.
  • Identify quality checks and safety practices and relate them to activities observed during site visits.
  • Use AutoCAD, SketchUp and BIM tools in construction workflows.
  • Prepare for job interviews and identify the first steps towards starting a construction business.
