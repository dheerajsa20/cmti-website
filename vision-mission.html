Vision and mission

Why CMTI exists and how we help people build careers in construction.

  • Technical foundations
  • Practitioner-led training
  • Project applications

Vision

Our objective is to help construction based professionals be prepared and ready once they enter the industry by providing an extensive module of necessary training that gives them the fundamental skills and paramount level of preparedness to perform tasks and tackle any challenge that lies ahead of them once they enter the building and construction industry.

Mission

Our services are designed to help individuals improve their chances of building their careers in the construction industry in two different ways. Firstly by providing the required training to be eligible for employment. Secondly by offering placements and opportunities to professionals through our list of excellent, renowned clients and contacts in the construction industry.
