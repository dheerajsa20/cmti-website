Corporate training

Capability Development for Staff

When skilled hires are hard to find, upskilling helps existing staff take on more responsibility in their current roles, while reskilling prepares them for new roles. CMTI works with L&D and Operations to identify skill gaps and design training around the work participants will do.

Training by career stage

  1. Freshers

    Diploma Engineer Trainee (DET) and Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET) programmes. We tailor the duration and topics to business needs. CMTI has delivered GET programmes for nine years, with programme details available on request.

  2. Middle management

    3–15 years of experience

    We recommend a two-day workshop on construction topics selected with your team.

  3. Construction and project managers

    15+ years of experience

    Workshops tailored to senior project responsibilities and organisational priorities.
