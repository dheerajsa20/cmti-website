How CMTI builds a training programme

Five steps with your L&D and operations teams, led by CMTI’s 50+ practitioner trainers. Scope, duration and format are set to your priorities and budget.

Where the training happens

At your offices and on live sites across India, with online and hybrid options.

  1. Staff programmes at offices and sites

    Upskilling for current roles and reskilling for new ones, designed around the work participants do.

  2. Practice on live sites

    Hybrid programmes add site-based practice to online sessions and in-person workshops.

  3. Trainee engineer programmes

    DET and GET programmes with the duration and topics set to your needs, run by CMTI for nine years.
