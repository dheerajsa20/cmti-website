Who should apply?

Eligibility

Diploma holders, graduates and postgraduates, including freshers and young professionals with one to two years of experience.

Target audience

Aspiring interior design professionals and early-career professionals interested in large-scale interior projects.

Programme objective

Learn to gather client requirements, prepare drawings and estimates, select materials and hardware, and understand how designs move from factory production to site installation.

Watch on YouTube

Syllabus

Hands-on exercises, software training and field visits to modular factories, showrooms and interior project sites.

01

Interior design standards, drawings and specifications

02

Materials and hardware selection for interior and modular works

03

AutoCAD, SketchUp and an introduction to BIM

04

Quantity estimation, basic costing and modular cut lists

05

Client requirements, design presentations and feedback

06

Factory production, showroom design and material selection, and site installation

Learning outcomes

  • Prepare basic AutoCAD layouts and SketchUp 3D models.
  • Interpret specifications and select materials and hardware.
  • Calculate quantities and prepare estimates and cut lists.
  • Explain how drawings guide manufacturing and installation.
  • Document client requirements, present options and respond to feedback.
