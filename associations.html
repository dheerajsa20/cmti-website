ACCE

Association of Consulting Civil Engineers

A forum for civil engineers to exchange practical knowledge on design, construction and professional practice.

ICI

Indian Concrete Institute

Promotes knowledge of concrete technology, from materials and mix design to site execution, testing and durability.

IPA

Indian Plumbing Association

Promotes good practice in plumbing, water supply and sanitation, including efficient use of water in buildings.

IGBC

Indian Green Building Council

Promotes green building practices, including energy efficiency, water conservation and responsible material use.
