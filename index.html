How the programmes run

  1. Staff programmes at your sites

    We map skill gaps with your L&D and operations teams, then train freshers, middle managers and project heads.

  2. Trade training for workmen

    Formwork, masonry, bar bending, plumbing, waterproofing and finishes, taught on site by trade trainers.

  3. Construction management course (CACM)

    Three months of drawings, BOQ, bar bending schedules, sequencing, quality and safety, with site visits.

  4. Interior design course (IDM)

    Three months of layouts, materials, estimates and cut lists, with visits to modular factories and showrooms.
