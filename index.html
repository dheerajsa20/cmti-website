About CMTI · Established 2018

Preparing professionals for construction careers

CMTI is one of India’s leading construction edtech institutes, delivering industry-led skilling across India.

Er. S. G. Ashok Kumar, PMP, founded the Construction Management Training Institute in 2018. With guidance from Er. V. Suresh, a senior industry leader in building and construction standards, CMTI has expanded its training and placement activities across India.

About Us

Construction skills for a developed India

NITI Aayog’s August 2026 skilling report emphasises flexible, lifelong learning aligned with industry needs. In construction, foundational skilling helps new entrants read drawings, measure work and follow site procedures. Reskilling helps experienced professionals move into roles that use BIM, digital quality tools and new construction methods. Upskilling helps project teams improve planning, safety, quality and resource efficiency as India builds towards Viksit Bharat 2047.

Source inspiration: NITI Aayog, Reimagining Skilling for Viksit Bharat@2047, August 2026.

Corporate, Government and International Training

CMTI is a technical training partner to real estate and engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) companies. It delivers capability development programmes at project sites across India, has trained the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), and delivered an international programme for Towell Group in Manchester, UK.

College Trainings and Placements

On-campus and off-campus training, internship support and campus interviews for engineering students. CMTI partners with 100+ engineering colleges, has placed 5,000+ engineers in construction and interiors roles, and works with 300+ companies across construction, real estate, interiors and BIM.
