Taught by people from the site

Er. S. G. Ashok Kumar, PMP, founded CMTI in 2018 after 26 years on projects including L&T highways and airports. CMTI now works with 50+ practitioner trainers, most of them former L&T professionals, based in Delhi NCR, Gujarat, Karnataka, Pune, Hyderabad and Chennai.

Beyond students, CMTI trains teams at real estate and EPC companies, has trained the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), and delivered an international programme for Towell Group in Manchester, UK.

What a CMTI MoU gives your college

CMTI works with 100+ engineering colleges on placements, training and curriculum, and has placed 5,000+ engineers with 300+ companies in construction, real estate, interiors and BIM.

  • Placements and campus interviews Employer coordination, résumé guidance and mock interviews
  • Internships Help finding and applying for industry placements
  • Software training On-campus CAD, BIM, planning and estimation practice
  • Competitive exams Preparation guidance and practice assessments
  • Free courses Selected technical and career-readiness courses
  • Curriculum input Board of Studies contributions on electives, labs and projects
  • Faculty and practical training Workshops, site visits, expert lectures and webinars
  • Campus green audits Assessing and improving environmental performance
  • NBA accreditation visits Industry-partner input on internships and collaboration
